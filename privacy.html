Privacy Policy

SAFU Live  ·  Effective date: November 2, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 2, 2026

SAFU Live ("SAFU Live", "we", "us", "our") operates the SAFU Live mobile application (Android package com.believoo.app) and related services at iyolme.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our live-streaming, audio-room, short-video and social features, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using SAFU Live you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • Account data — phone number (for OTP sign-in) or your Google account name/email/photo when you sign in with Google; the username, display name, avatar, gender, date of birth and bio you add to your profile.
  • Content you create — live video/audio streams, audio-room participation, reels, posts, stories, photos, comments, voice and text messages, gifts sent, and profile/customisation assets you upload.
  • Transactions — coin-pack purchases, VIP subscriptions, gifts sent and received, host earnings ("Beans"), redemption and withdrawal details you submit (e.g. payout account identifiers). Card and payment instrument details are processed by Google Play Billing — we never see or store your full card number.
  • Communications — support requests, feedback, surveys, and reports you send us, plus any information submitted through our account-deletion form.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device & identifiers — device model, OS version, Android ID / install ID, IP address, language, time zone, advertising ID (where available), and install-referrer data.
  • Usage data — rooms joined, streams watched, features used, taps and session duration, crash logs and diagnostics (via Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Streaming metadata — stream quality, channel, role (host/viewer), and connection diagnostics needed to run live audio/video.
  • Approximate location — inferred from IP; precise location only if you grant the location permission (e.g. to tag a reel).

2. On-device processing

Host-safety and camera features (face presence detection, beauty/AR effects) run on your device using ML Kit / the camera pipeline. Facial images are analysed locally to keep streams compliant and render effects — we do not upload, store, or build a biometric identifier from your face.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the service — sign-in, matchmaking, streaming, chat, wallet, VIP.
  • Power the economy — coin purchases, gifts, host earnings, withdrawals, fraud checks.
  • Safety & moderation — detect violations, enforce our Terms, respond to reports and bans.
  • Personalisation — recommendations, rankings, content feeds, language.
  • Communications — service messages, security alerts, support replies, optional marketing.
  • Analytics & product improvement — reliability, crash fixing, feature usage.
  • Legal — tax/accounting records for payouts, lawful requests, protecting our rights.

4. Live streams & public content

Anything you broadcast or post publicly — live video, audio rooms, reels, profile details, comments — can be seen, recorded and shared by other users and by us for moderation. Never share information in a stream that you are not comfortable being public.

5. Who we share information with

  • Other users — your public profile, content, room activity and gift events are visible per app features.
  • Service providers (processors) — Agora (live audio/video transport), Google Firebase (authentication, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics), Google Play Billing (payments & subscriptions), Google AdMob (ads), hosting/CDN providers (iyolme.com infrastructure). They process data only to deliver their service to us.
  • Payout partners — withdrawal/payment processors for host earnings.
  • Law & safety — regulators, courts, or authorities where legally required, or to protect users, investigate fraud, or enforce our Terms.
  • Business transfers — if SAFU Live is merged, acquired or reorganised, data may transfer with notice where required.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Retention & deletion

  • We keep account data while your account is active and as needed for safety, fraud-prevention and legal obligations (e.g. payout tax records).
  • You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Settings → Delete account) or via our deletion request page. Verified requests are processed within 30 days; residual backups and legally-required records are removed on their normal cycle.
  • Public content may persist in caches or third-party copies made by other users during broadcast — this is outside our control.

7. Security

We use TLS encryption in transit, access controls, and signed API requests. No service is 100% secure — keep your OTPs and session private and report suspicious activity to support@iyolme.com.

8. Children

SAFU Live is for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. International transfers

Our infrastructure and processors may handle data in countries other than yours. We take reasonable steps to protect data wherever it is processed, as described here.

10. Your rights & choices

  • Edit profile data in-app; control permissions (camera, mic, location) from system settings.
  • Opt out of marketing pushes in notification settings.
  • Request access, correction or deletion via support@iyolme.com — we respond within 30 days.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change and material changes will be flagged in-app. Continued use after an update means acceptance.
